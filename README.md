# Core Webber — Property, Renovation & Construction Website

A responsive public website prototype for Core Webber's property and small construction services.

## Services presented
- Property sales and rentals
- Home renovations and property improvements
- Small construction and building works
- Plumbing
- Painting and finishing
- Electrical service coordination
- Building plans and drawing coordination

## Files
- `index.html` — website structure and content
- `styles.css` — responsive visual design
- `script.js` — mobile navigation, year display and enquiry-form prototype

## Before production launch
1. Confirm the official business name presentation and logo.
2. Add the approved company phone number, email address and service area.
3. Connect the enquiry form to a secure email service, CRM or backend. The current form is a front-end prototype and does not transmit or store enquiries.
4. Add real property listings only after availability, pricing, photos and listing permissions are confirmed.
5. Confirm contractor credentials, electrical compliance and any required building approvals before advertising regulated work.
6. Add privacy notice, cookie notice if applicable, analytics consent and search metadata for the confirmed service area.

The property images are illustrative stock photography and are not property listings.
