WHO WE ARE

Property opportunities. Practical project support.

Pace 2 Find Properties is the property-facing brand and online platform operated under Core Webber (Pty) Ltd. We provide a central point for people and businesses looking to buy, sell or rent property, as well as clients planning property improvements or construction work.

Our approach brings property enquiries and project requirements together. We help clients communicate what they need, explore relevant opportunities and connect with suitable service providers. Each enquiry is considered according to its location, scope, timing and applicable professional or regulatory requirements.
